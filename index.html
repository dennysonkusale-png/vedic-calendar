Festival dates for 2026 and 2027 are cross-checked against widely used panchangs for India. Because Hindu festivals follow a lunar tithi, dates can shift by a day between regions and between Smarta and Vaishnava traditions — where sources differ, the more widely observed date is shown. Panchang values for today are computed in your browser from low-precision Sun and Moon positions and are approximate; please confirm ritual timings with your local panchang or priest.

A note on sources

Scriptural links (Purana, Veda, Samhita, Upanishad, epic) are given as the traditional basis of each observance. Where an account is folklore or later custom rather than scripture, it is described as such. The Vedas, Puranas and epics are vast and layered; these are short introductions, not the last word.
